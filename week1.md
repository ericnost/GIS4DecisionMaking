# Decision-Making vs Decision-Support
- GIS is not (or should not be) a device that makes decisions
  - You have to know what you want to get out of it
  - It can reveal unknowns and new information
  - It can help us be very efficient with resources ($, time, etc.)
- But it’s not going to tell you whether you have the right goals
- GIS is always part of some process

# "Location Intelligence" for Business

- Siting (e.g. locating a store)
- Operations (e.g. delivery routes, supply chains)
- Finance (e.g. investments)
> Increasingly, imagery can be effectively utilized for business purposes.  For instance, companies such as Zonda can utilize imagery to determine business processes on the ground, such as the status of building construction from day-to-day, the hourly retail market activity based on images of number of cars in the parking lots of retail stores, and changes in seaport supply chains based on the loading and unloading of containers from ships and tractor-trailers.

# Geospatial Careers
- GIS technician, [example](https://www.hillsborough.net/DocumentCenter/View/2509/GIS-Technician-?bidId=)
> This position is responsible as the lead in collecting geospatial data from the field through the Town’s geodatabase or through other publicly available sources, and using that data to prepare visual representations including, but not limited to, maps, publications or spreadsheets
- GIS analyst, [example](https://community.esri.com/en/discussion/914463/gis-analyst)
> The purpose of this position is to support the users of Geographic Information Systems (GIS). Coordinate efforts and work as liaison between the Geospatial Services staff, Information Technology, and the other user divisions and departments for development and implementation of interactive mapping applications and spatial analytics
- GIS programmer/developer, [example](https://www.ccgisc.org/AdminDocs/GIS_Programmer.pdf)
> Responsible for the design, development and deployment of applications, including spatially enabled web-based mapping applications and dynamically driven web pages
- GIS manager, [example](https://cob.org/wp-content/uploads/ITGISManager.jd.pdf)
> Leads and manages the City’s GIS program providing vision and direction to city staff, leadership in the utilization of GIS and promoting GIS effectively as a business solution. Manages the implementation, development, and services delivery of the GIS including strategic planning, coordination, prioritization, and goal setting

# ArcGIS Online (AGOL)
What is it?
> Most notably a way to making, interacting with, and sharing maps (i.e. Map Viewer)
> ArcGIS Online is more than a tool for making maps because it includes access to curated and un-curated data resources (LivingAtlas), a wide variety of analytic tools (e.g. geocoding / credits), as well as being a hub for hosting and sharing spatial data (feature servers), and also a platform for communicating in various ways (e.g. dashboards) (custom applications)

---
## Why AGOL?
- Coordination across enterprise, publics
- Cross-platform
- Why _not_ AGOL:
> Tasks that ArcGIS Online does not do well include the creation of static, cartographically complex maps; customized analytic tools except by exporting data to a third-party application; and management of complex relational datasets.

---
## How AGOL?
> A **Hosted Feature Layer** is a type of content that is used to store vector data (i.e., points, lines and polygons). Along with the spatial data, Hosted Feature Layers contain attribute information of the features represented and can be shared through popups. Users can create a Hosted Feature Layer while signed onto ArcGIS. Users of ArcGIS Pro or ArcGIS Desktop are able to publish directly from their desktop software to ArcGIS Online. A benefit of this method is that all the symbology and scale dependencies defined in the desktop version are transferred to the Hosted Feature Layer created in their content on ArcGIS Online. Once published to ArcGIS Online, these layers can then again be consumed by desktop GIS software, as well as web-based applications if the sharing settings are appropriately assigned.

- This is what you created!
- Fuller example: [EJSCREEN](https://pedp-ejscreen.azurewebsites.net/)
