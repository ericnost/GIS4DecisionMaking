# Week 4: GIS for Decision-Making
## Navigating Client Requests for Proposals (RFPs)

Today's focus: Translating stakeholder needs into spatial workflows.

---

# What is an RFP?

A Request for Proposal (RFP) is a formal document issued by a client (e.g., a municipality or private developer) seeking bids from consultants. 

* **Objective:** Defines the problem that needs solving.
* **Scope of Work:** Outlines the required deliverables (maps, databases, web apps).
* **Evaluation Criteria:** How your spatial analysis proposal will be judged.

---

# Analyzing the Stakeholders

When drafting a consultant response, you must map out your audience before you map out the data.

1. **Primary Client:** The agency funding the project.
2. **Regulatory Bodies:** Authorities ensuring compliance (e.g., environmental protections).
3. **The Public:** Community members impacted by the proposed development.

> *"A flawless spatial analysis is useless if it does not directly address the concerns of the stakeholders reviewing it."*
